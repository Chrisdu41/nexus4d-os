# Strategic Fit Gate v0.8

Before committing resources to a channel, format or tactic, evaluate strategic fit.

## Status
- `CORE` — central to the validated strategy
- `SUPPORTING` — reinforces a core channel
- `EXPERIMENTAL` — justified test with bounded investment
- `REJECTED` — poor fit or unacceptable opportunity cost

## Evaluation
1. Audience-channel fit
2. Offer-channel fit
3. Client capability fit
4. Format capability fit
5. Measurement capability
6. Evidence already available
7. Opportunity cost
8. Reversibility of the test

## Output
Return status, rationale, assumptions, risks and what evidence could change the status.

A status other than CORE does not automatically block a mission. It changes scope, investment and confidence.
