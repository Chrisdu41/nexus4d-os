# Principles

1. Evidence before confidence.
2. Hypothesis before asset.
3. Learning before scaling.
4. One dominant idea per creative.
5. Proof before superlative.
6. Client language before marketing jargon.
7. Distinctiveness before decoration.
8. Reusable knowledge before duplicated prompts.
9. Human accountability for consequential decisions.
10. Market feedback outranks model opinion.
